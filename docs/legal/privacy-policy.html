Amiro Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-28

This policy describes what Amiro ("the App") does with your information. Amiro is built to keep your data on your own device. There is no Amiro server, no account system, and no analytics or advertising SDK in the App.

Who this covers

Amiro is published by Developer's Coffee. This policy applies to the Amiro mobile app on Android and iOS. It does not cover any third-party app, website, or service you reach by tapping a link inside Amiro (for example, a link you added to your own profile).

What information the App handles

When you set up your Amiro identity, you may enter:

  • Display name and username
  • A short bio
  • Mobile number, email address
  • Social handles (X/Twitter, Instagram) and a personal website URL

All of this is stored only on your device, in a local database. It is never sent to Developer's Coffee or to any third party by the App itself.

Your avatar (body, hair, outfit, and other cosmetic choices) is also stored only on your device.

Sharing with another person

Amiro's core feature is sharing your profile with someone else, by:

  • Showing them a QR code, or scanning theirs
  • Tapping phones together (NFC), on Android

Before you share, you choose which fields are public. Only the fields you mark public are included in what you show or send — anything you leave private never leaves your device. The other person's phone reads that information directly from the QR code or NFC tap; it does not pass through any server Developer's Coffee operates, because none exists.

If you scan someone else's QR code or receive their NFC tap, whatever public profile information they chose to share is stored on your device, the same way your own profile is. You control that data (including deleting it) on your device.

Camera and NFC

  • The App requests camera access only to scan a QR code someone shows you. No photo or video is captured, stored, or transmitted — the camera feed is used solely to detect a QR code.
  • The App requests NFC access only to read an incoming tap-to-share, and (Android only) to let your phone emulate a tag so someone else can read your shared profile via NFC. No other NFC use.

What the App does not do

  • No account creation, no sign-in, no password.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs.
  • No network requests of any kind, from the App to Developer's Coffee or anyone else.
  • No location data is collected.
  • No data is sold or shared with third parties, because none is collected by us in the first place.

Your control over your data

Because everything lives on your device:

  • Editing or clearing a field in your identity removes it immediately.
  • Uninstalling the App removes all Amiro data stored by it, subject to your device's own OS behavior (for example, an OS-level backup you created yourself, which is outside the App's control).
  • There is no Amiro account to delete, because there is no Amiro account.

Children's privacy

Amiro is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from children. Since all data stays on-device and nothing is transmitted to us, we have no way to collect such information in the first place.

Changes to this policy

If Amiro's data practices change (for example, if a future version adds optional cloud backup), this policy will be updated first, and that version will describe the change clearly, including whether it is optional.
