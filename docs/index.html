How it works

Build it once. Share it everywhere.

The whole loop happens on your phone — nothing round-trips to a server.

01

Build your identity

Set a name, bio, and contact details — each with its own public/private toggle — then build a 3D avatar: hair, outfit, accessories, rotate it a full 360° to see every angle.

02

Share it

Show a QR code, or — on Android — tap phones together. The other person sees only the fields you marked public. Nothing passes through a server, because none exists.

03

Collect who you meet

Every person you scan or tap is logged in your Discovery Passport — who you met, when, and what they were wearing at the time.

04

Grow your wardrobe

Visit the Store for new hairstyles, outfits, and accessories — common pieces free, rarer pieces in numbered series, worth showing off.

Features

Everything the loop needs, nothing it doesn't.

◐

3D avatar, 360°

Drag to rotate. Hair, outfits, and accessories rendered in real time on your device.

▦

Tap or scan sharing

NFC tap-to-share on Android, QR code on every platform. No pairing, no waiting.

◈

Field-level privacy

Email, phone, socials, website — each one is public or private, your choice alone.

▤

Discovery Passport

A private log of everyone you've met through Amiro, and what they were wearing.

✦

Rarity & series

Cosmetics come in tiers, some in numbered series — a few free, some worth collecting.

◍

Nothing leaves your phone

No analytics, no ads, no account. Uninstalling removes everything Amiro stored.

Why it looks like this

“A wearable digital identity styled as a status object — one brass accent does all the emphasis work, and nothing is decorated for decoration's sake.”
Amiro is built for premium, cult-status positioning: people collect it because it feels worth showing off, not because they're locked out of a free experience.

Built local-first, on purpose

There is no Amiro server. Your identity, avatar, and Discovery Passport are stored only on your device. Sharing sends data directly, phone to phone.
